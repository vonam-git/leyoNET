## What it is about

In module 145 an existing network is operated and extended. The focus is on VLANs: separating departments, trunks between floors, routing between VLANs with router on a stick or a layer 3 switch, DHCP via a relay and finally a clean network design with redundancy.

The series starts with the “old-school network” with one router per network and ends with three-tier and collapsed core. The extra tasks practise troubleshooting, management VLAN and port channel.

## Theory in this order

1. [[ethernet-mac]] and [[switch]]: how a switch learns and forwards.
2. [[vlan]]: access and trunk ports, the 802.1Q tag, native VLAN.
3. [[router]] and [[layer3-switch]]: routing between VLANs.
4. [[acl]]: restricting access between VLANs.
5. [[dhcp]]: pools on the layer 3 switch and relay to a central server.
6. [[stp]] and [[lacp]]: redundant links without loops.
7. [[netzwerkdesign]]: core, distribution and access.

## Teaching tips

- UE05 “Who receives the broadcast?” works as a theory check before the trunk: guess first, then watch it in the simulation.
- In the rack view, ports have the colour of their VLAN; wrongly assigned ports stand out immediately.
- The showcase “Data centre network” shows a whole design with core, distribution and access for presenting.
